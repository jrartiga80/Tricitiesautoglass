Same-day windshield replacement is sometimes possible and sometimes not. A few plain things decide it.

What decides same-day windshield replacement

You cracked the glass this morning and need the car by tonight. Here is what stands between you and that:

  • The right glass. The part has to match your VIN. Sensors, heating and camera brackets all change it.
  • Room in the schedule. The job itself runs 60 to 90 minutes for most vehicles.
  • The drive-away wait. The adhesive needs time before the car is safe to drive. That time is set by the adhesive and the weather.
  • Camera recalibration. Cars with a forward camera may need this step after the glass goes in. It adds time.
  • Weather and daylight. Cold, rain and snow affect how the adhesive sets.
  • Insurance steps. A claim can add a phone call or two before the work starts.

How to get the earliest appointment

  1. Call early. The earlier in the day you call, the more room there is.
  2. Have your VIN ready. It lets us match the glass without guessing. You will find it on the plate at the base of the windshield or on the driver's door jamb sticker.
  3. Tell us your features. Camera near the mirror? Rain sensor? Heated edge?
  4. Be flexible on location. We come to your home, work or job site. Tell us where the car will be.
  5. Say how soon you need it. If you have to drive tonight, tell us so we plan around it.

We do not promise a time before we know your vehicle. Call (509) 581-2623 and we will tell you the earliest time we can get to you.

Same-day still means a drive-away wait

A fast install does not skip the cure. The new glass is held in by urethane adhesive, and the adhesive needs time to build strength. Until it does, the windshield may not hold in a crash.

Ask for a specific time before the technician leaves. In cold weather the wait can run longer. Our safe drive-away time guide has the details.

If it cannot happen today

  • If a crack blocks your view, do not drive. We come to you.
  • For a chip, put clear tape over it and keep dirt out.
  • Warm the car slowly. Our defroster guide explains why.
  • Drive gently and skip potholes if you can.

Can you replace my windshield today?+
It depends on the glass for your vehicle, the schedule, and the weather. Call us with your VIN and we will tell you the earliest time we can get to you.
How long does the replacement itself take?+
Most jobs run 60 to 90 minutes. Then the car has to wait out the safe drive-away time.
Can I drive right after a same-day replacement?+
Not right away. Wait for the drive-away time your installer gives you. It depends on the adhesive and the temperature.
What do you need to quote a same-day job?+
Your year, make and model, the VIN, any camera or sensor near the mirror, and where the car is parked.
Does insurance slow down a same-day job?+
A claim can add a call or two before the work starts. Washington lets you pick your own shop, so you can book with us first and ask your insurer how to proceed.
