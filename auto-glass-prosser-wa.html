Is there a trip charge for Prosser?+
We schedule Prosser as part of a route rather than a special trip, so in most cases there is no extra charge. If your timing is tight and we have to make a dedicated run, we will tell you the cost up front rather than surprising you on the invoice.
How fast can you get to me in Prosser?+
Same-day is often possible when you call in the morning, and next-day is normal. Chip repairs are the quickest — under 30 minutes on site. Windshield replacements run 60 to 90 minutes plus adhesive cure time before the vehicle is safe to drive.
Do I need to bring the car anywhere?+
No. We are a mobile service, so we come to your home, workplace, or wherever the vehicle is parked in Prosser. All we need is reasonable access to the vehicle and, in bad weather, a garage or covered spot.
Do you bill insurance for Prosser customers?+
