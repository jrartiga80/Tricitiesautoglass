Dust and sunroof leaks in Kennewick

Most sunroof leaks come from clogged drain channels, not the glass. Dust builds up in the channels around the opening, and water that cannot drain ends up inside the car. If you see a wet headliner, call before the stain spreads.

What we handle

  • Fixed and sliding sunroof glass on cars, SUVs, and trucks.
  • Panoramic roof panels — front sliding panel and the fixed rear pane.
  • Shattered tempered panels, including full cleanup of granules from the headliner channel and interior.
  • Seals, weatherstrip, and drain channels when a roof is leaking but the glass is intact.
  • Track, cable, and motor diagnosis when the panel binds or will not close.

My sunroof shattered on its own. How?+
Panoramic panels are tempered glass under tension across a large span, and a small edge chip or a stress point in the frame can let go without an impact. It is more common than most drivers expect, particularly on large panoramic roofs.
Can a cracked sunroof be repaired with resin?+
No. Resin repair only works on laminated glass, and sunroof panels are tempered. A cracked panel gets replaced.
