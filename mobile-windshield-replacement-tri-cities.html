Mobile windshield replacement means the technician brings the glass and the tools to you. There is no shop to drive to and no waiting room.

How mobile windshield replacement works

Most jobs run 60 to 90 minutes. You do not have to stand there for it. The car does need to stay put afterward while the adhesive cures. The steps look like this:

  1. Match the glass. We use your VIN to pick the right part. Rain sensor, heated wiper park, acoustic layer, camera bracket and tint band all change it.
  2. Protect the car. Fenders, dash and cowl get covered before anything is cut.
  3. Remove the old glass. The old adhesive bead is cut and trimmed to a clean, even base.
  4. Prime and set. Fresh primer goes on, then a continuous bead of urethane, then the new glass.
  5. Reinstall the trim and sensors. Cars with a forward camera may need recalibration.
  6. Give you the drive-away time. It depends on the adhesive and the weather that day.

Where we can do it

Your driveway, your work parking lot, or a job site all work. Pick a flat spot with room to open the doors and move around the car. A garage is fine too.

We serve Kennewick, Richland, Pasco, Prosser and Sunnyside.

What winter does to a mobile job

Cold, dry air slows the adhesive. I-CAR notes that the shortest cure times assume 75 degrees and 50 percent humidity. Colder or drier air means a longer wait before you can drive.

Rain, snow and blowing dust are a bigger problem while the glass is being set. Tell us what the weather looks like when you call. A garage or covered spot can help, and sometimes the better move is a different time. Read more in our safe drive-away time guide.

Cars with a camera behind the glass

Many newer cars have a driver-assist camera mounted to the windshield. After a replacement, most makers call for recalibration. Some vehicles need a static setup indoors on level floor with a target board. Others need a road drive. A few need both.

Which one your car needs is set by the manufacturer, and we look it up by VIN before your appointment. Ask us how your car is handled. Our ADAS recalibration page has the details.

Get ready for your appointment

  • Clear the dash and the area around the windshield.
  • Park where the car can sit for the cure time.
  • Have the VIN and your insurance details ready.
  • Ask for the safe drive-away time before the technician leaves.
  • Ask about the workmanship warranty.
