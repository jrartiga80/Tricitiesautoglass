How to reach us

Phone: (509) 581-2623 — fastest way to get a quote and a time slot. Call or text.

Hours: Call for current hours. Fleet and commercial customers can call to ask about scheduling.

Service area: Kennewick, Richland, Pasco, Prosser and Sunnyside, plus West Richland, Benton City, Burbank, Finley and the surrounding Benton, Franklin and Yakima county areas. Call for a free quote and we will confirm we serve your address.

What to have ready

  • Year, make and model of the vehicle — and the VIN if you have it handy
  • Which piece of glass is damaged, and roughly how big the damage is
  • Whether the vehicle has lane keeping, adaptive cruise or automatic braking (that means camera recalibration)
  • Your insurance carrier and claim number, if you are filing a claim
  • Where the vehicle will be parked and for how long

Emergency and after-hours

A broken side or rear window leaves the vehicle unsecured, so those calls go to the front of the line. If it is after hours, call anyway and leave a message with your location — we will get weather sheeting on the opening and schedule the glass as early as the next morning.

Insurance claims

We can help with the claim. If you have not filed yet, call us before the carrier — we will tell you whether the damage is a repair or a replacement and whether a claim is even worth filing against your deductible. See how glass claims work in Washington.
