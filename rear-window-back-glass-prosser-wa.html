Gravel, farm traffic and back glass near Prosser

Gravel on I-82 and farm roads can break rear glass. A rock that only chips a windshield can shatter tempered glass into small pieces. If it happens, cover the opening and keep the car out of the rain until it is fixed.

Tempered glass behaves differently

Your windshield is laminated: two layers of glass bonded to a plastic interlayer, which is why it cracks and stays in one piece. Every other window on the vehicle is tempered, engineered to break into small dull-edged granules rather than shards.

That is safer in a collision and messier afterward. A shattered back glass puts glass in the cargo area, in the seat tracks, in the seat belt reels, and inside the door cavity. Cleanup is genuinely half the job, and it is the half most people underestimate.
