Heavy truck glass is its own trade

Class 8 windshields are large, heavy, and usually set into a rubber gasket rather than bonded, with a locking strip that has to be seated correctly around the full perimeter. Many sleepers run a two-piece windshield, so a single cracked half is often a half-price job — worth asking about before you assume the whole set.

We work on conventional and cab-over trucks, day cabs and sleepers, dump and mixer trucks, and vocational bodies. Vent glass, door glass, and rear cab windows too.

Downtime and DOT

A cracked windshield in the driver's viewing area is an out-of-service risk, and a truck sidelined at inspection costs far more than the glass. If you have a unit heading into an inspection or a run, say so and we will schedule around it.

We come to you along the I-82, I-182, US 395, and SR 240 corridors, and out to the terminals and yards in Pasco, Kennewick, Richland, Prosser, and Sunnyside. Most single-piece heavy truck windshields go in inside two hours.

What to have ready when you call

  • Make, model, and year of the tractor
  • Whether it is a one-piece or two-piece windshield, and which side is damaged
  • Whether the glass is tinted, shaded, or has a heated element
  • Where the truck is parked and how long it will be there

Can you replace just one half of a two-piece semi windshield?+
Yes, and it is usually the right call. If only the driver's side is cracked, replacing that half alone saves you real money — provided the other half is not pitted enough to be a visibility problem on night runs.
How long is a semi truck windshield replacement?+
Plan on about two hours for most units, including seating the gasket and locking strip properly. Two-piece sets and trucks with heated or heavily shaded glass can run longer.
Do you come to truck stops and terminals?+
Yes. We service the I-82, I-182, US 395, and SR 240 corridors and the yards and terminals across the Tri-Cities. Give us the location and a window when the truck will be sitting.
Can a cracked windshield put my truck out of service?+
It can, depending on where the damage is and whether cracks intersect. Damage in the driver's viewing area is the one that gets trucks flagged. Fix it before the inspection, not after.
