My sunroof shattered on its own. How?+
Panoramic panels are tempered glass under tension across a large span, and a small edge chip or a stress point in the frame can let go without an impact. It is more common than most drivers expect, particularly on large panoramic roofs.
Can a cracked sunroof be repaired with resin?+
No. Resin repair only works on laminated glass, and sunroof panels are tempered. A cracked panel gets replaced.
