Common Questions

Auto Glass FAQ

Does my insurance cover windshield replacement?+
Many comprehensive policies cover glass repair and replacement. Washington does not require insurers to waive the deductible on glass claims, but many policies carry a separate, lower glass deductible, and many carriers waive it entirely for a chip repair. Call us and we will help you check coverage before any work starts.
Can a cracked windshield be repaired, or does it need replacing?+
Chips smaller than a quarter and cracks shorter than about six inches can usually be repaired with resin injection. Larger cracks, damage reaching the edge of the glass, or anything in the driver’s direct line of sight typically requires a full replacement. We give you an honest assessment — no pressure to upgrade.
How long does a windshield replacement take?+
Most replacements take 60 to 90 minutes start to finish. Afterward we give you a specific safe drive-away time based on the adhesive and the weather that day, rather than a generic guess.
Do you come to my home or do I drive to you?+
We come to you. Our mobile units handle repairs and replacements at your home, workplace, or any parking lot across the Tri-Cities. There is no shop to drive to.
What areas do you serve?+
Kennewick, Richland, Pasco, Prosser, and Sunnyside, plus West Richland, Benton City, Burbank, Finley, and the surrounding Benton, Franklin, and Yakima county areas. Not sure whether we cover your location? Just call.
Do I need ADAS recalibration after a windshield replacement?+
If your vehicle has lane keeping, adaptive cruise, or automatic emergency braking, then yes — the forward-facing camera is aimed relative to the glass and has to be recalibrated once the glass is replaced. It is included in our quote for ADAS-equipped vehicles.
