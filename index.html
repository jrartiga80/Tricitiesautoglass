Common Questions

Auto Glass FAQ

Does my insurance cover windshield replacement?+
Many comprehensive auto policies cover glass repair and replacement with no out-of-pocket cost. Washington doesn’t waive the deductible on glass claims, but many policies have a separate, lower glass deductible. Call us and we’ll help you check coverage before any work starts.
Can a cracked windshield be repaired, or does it need replacing?+
Chips smaller than a quarter and cracks shorter than about 6 inches can usually be repaired with resin injection. Larger cracks, or anything in the driver’s direct line of sight, typically require a full replacement. We give you an honest assessment — no pressure to upgrade.
How long does a windshield replacement take?+
Most replacements take 60–90 minutes start to finish. Afterward we recommend leaving the car parked for at least an hour to let the adhesive cure fully. We’ll give you the exact safe-drive-away time based on conditions that day.
Do you come to my home or do I drive to you?+
We come to you. Our mobile units are fully equipped to handle repairs and replacements at your home, workplace, or any parking lot in the Tri-Cities. There’s no shop to drive to — that’s the whole point.
What areas do you serve?+
We primarily serve Kennewick, Richland, and Pasco, plus West Richland, Benton City, and Prosser. Not sure whether we cover your location? Just call and we’ll tell you right away.
Is the glass you use as good as the original?+
Yes. We use OEM-equivalent glass that meets the same safety and fit specs as factory glass. For newer vehicles with ADAS cameras in the windshield, we also handle proper recalibration so your safety systems work correctly after replacement.
