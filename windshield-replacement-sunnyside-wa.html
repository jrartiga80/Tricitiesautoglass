When replacement is the right call

A windshield is a structural part of your vehicle. It supports the roof in a rollover and it is the backstop the passenger airbag inflates against. That is why the repair-or-replace line is not about looks. If the damage compromises the laminate, the glass has to be replaced.

We replace rather than repair when a crack runs longer than about six inches, when damage sits in the driver's primary line of sight, when a chip has spread to the edge of the glass, or when there are three or more separate impact points. If your damage is smaller than that, say so on the phone — a resin repair costs a fraction of a replacement and we would rather do the cheaper job.

How a mobile replacement works

The whole job runs 60 to 90 minutes for most vehicles. You do not need to be standing there for it, but the car does need to stay put afterward while the urethane cures.

How quickly can you handle windshield replacement in Sunnyside?+
Call in the morning and same-day is often possible; next-day is normal. Sunnyside is part of our regular route, so you are not waiting on a special trip.
How long until I can drive after a windshield replacement?+
Most vehicles are safe to drive within an hour of the set, but the exact safe drive-away time depends on the adhesive and the temperature and humidity that day. We give you a specific time before we leave — not a guess.
Can you replace a windshield in cold or windy weather?+
Usually yes. Urethane cures more slowly in cold weather, so the safe drive-away time gets longer. In heavy rain or high wind we will ask to work in your garage or reschedule — a contaminated bond line is not worth the convenience.
