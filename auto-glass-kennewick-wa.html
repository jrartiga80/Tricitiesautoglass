Mobile auto glass service in Kennewick

Kennewick is where most of our chip repairs come from, and the reason is the road network. I-82 and the 395 blue bridge approach push a lot of gravel around, and the stretch of Clearwater Avenue and 27th through the commercial core keeps trucks and construction traffic moving all day. A rock off a truck tire on the I-82 grade is the single most common story we hear on the phone.

Because so much of Kennewick works out of the Columbia Center corridor and the medical and retail districts around it, most of our Kennewick appointments happen in a parking lot while the customer is at work. You leave the keys or you meet us at the car — either way, nobody spends an afternoon in a waiting room.
