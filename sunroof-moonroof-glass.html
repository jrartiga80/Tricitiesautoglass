Sunroof glass is a sealing job, not just a glass job

Anyone can drop a panel into an opening. What determines whether you are back next winter with a wet headliner is the seal, the drain channels, and whether the panel sits flush in its tracks at both ends of travel.

We replace the perimeter seal with the glass, clear the four corner drains that carry water down the A and C pillars, and cycle the roof through its full range to confirm the panel seats evenly and the tilt and slide functions are correct.

What we handle

  • Fixed and sliding sunroof glass on cars, SUVs, and trucks.
  • Panoramic roof panels — front sliding panel and the fixed rear pane.
  • Shattered tempered panels, including full cleanup of granules from the headliner channel and interior.
  • Seals, weatherstrip, and drain channels when a roof is leaking but the glass is intact.
  • Track, cable, and motor diagnosis when the panel binds or will not close.

Parts and timing

Sunroof panels are more often ordered than stocked, especially panoramic glass, so plan on a day or two for the part on most vehicles. We will confirm availability by VIN when you call and give you a real date rather than a hopeful one.

If your roof is open to the sky right now, tell us on the phone. We will get a weather-tight temporary cover on it the same day where we can.

My sunroof shattered on its own. How?+
Panoramic panels are tempered glass under tension across a large span, and a small edge chip or a stress point in the frame can let go without an impact. It is more common than most drivers expect, particularly on large panoramic roofs.
My sunroof leaks but the glass looks fine. Can you help?+
Usually the seal has hardened or the corner drains are plugged with pine needles and dust, which backs water up into the headliner. That is a service visit, not a glass replacement, and it costs a lot less.
Can a cracked sunroof be repaired with resin?+
No. Resin repair only works on laminated glass, and sunroof panels are tempered. A cracked panel gets replaced.
Is sunroof glass covered by insurance?+
Comprehensive coverage generally treats it the same as any other glass claim. Whether your deductible makes a claim worth filing depends on the panel cost, and panoramic glass is expensive enough that it usually is. We will quote it both ways.
