Foggy Windshield on the Inside in Winter

A foggy windshield is a safety problem. Here is how to clear it without hurting the glass.

A foggy windshield inside your car happens when moist air meets cold glass. The moisture turns to tiny drops on the surface.

Why the inside fogs up

You and your passengers breathe out moisture. Wet coats, damp floor mats and a coffee cup add more. In winter the glass is cold, so that moisture lands on it and blocks your view.

How to clear it fast

AAA suggests a simple winter routine. Set the climate control to full heat and aim the vents at the glass. Turn recirculation off so drier air from outside comes in. You can read their full tips at AAA Club Alliance.

  1. Turn the fan up and aim the air at the windshield.
  2. Turn off air recirculation.
  3. Wipe the glass with a clean microfiber cloth if you need to see right now.
  4. Crack a window for a moment if the fog is heavy.

A note on frozen glass

If the outside of the glass is frozen too, do not use hot water. Let the defroster work gradually. Read why hot water on a frozen windshield is a bad idea and how a defroster can crack a windshield. Temperature shock is a real risk, especially with a chip. See why windshields crack in cold weather.

How to keep it from fogging

  • Keep the inside of the glass clean. Dirt gives moisture something to cling to.
  • Use an ammonia-free glass cleaner and a microfiber cloth. AAA recommends this too.
  • Shake snow off your coat and boots before you get in.
  • Take wet floor mats out to dry.
  • Try an anti-fog spray on clean, dry glass, and follow the label.

When fog is a sign of something else

If the fog keeps coming back or you see moisture between layers of glass, tell a professional. Water inside the cabin after rain can also point to a leak. If you had new glass installed recently, call your installer.

Should I use hot or cold air to clear fog?

Hot air in winter, aimed at the glass. Warm air holds more moisture, so it clears the fog. Turn recirculation off so drier outside air comes in.

Why does my windshield fog up more with passengers?

People breathe out moisture. More people means more moisture in a closed car.

Can wiping the inside make fog worse?

It can leave streaks, and you may have to wipe again. A clean microfiber cloth works best. A clean windshield also fogs less.

Can a defroster crack my windshield?

It can if hot air hits very cold glass, especially glass with a chip. Warm the car gradually.

Does a foggy windshield mean I need new glass?

Not by itself. Fog is moisture in the air. If you see cloudiness inside the glass layers or a leak, have it checked.
