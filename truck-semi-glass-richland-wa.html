How quickly can you handle truck & semi glass in Richland?+
Call in the morning and same-day is often possible; next-day is normal. Richland is part of our regular route, so you are not waiting on a special trip.
Can a cracked windshield put my truck out of service?+
It can, depending on where the damage is and whether cracks intersect. Damage in the driver's viewing area is the one that gets trucks flagged. Fix it before the inspection, not after.
How long is a semi truck windshield replacement?+
Plan on about two hours for most units, including seating the gasket and locking strip properly. Two-piece sets and trucks with heated or heavily shaded glass can run longer.
Can you recalibrate my vehicle's camera in Richland?+
Yes. Static calibrations are done indoors on a level floor with target boards; dynamic calibrations use a marked-lane road drive, and SR 240 and I-182 work well for that. We confirm the required procedure by VIN before the appointment.
