Can a Defroster Crack Your Windshield?

Heat is not the problem. Heat that arrives too fast is.

A defroster can crack a windshield, and it usually happens to glass that already has a chip.

How a defroster cracks a windshield

On a cold morning the outside of the glass is freezing. The defroster warms the inside. That gap puts stress on the glass. Caliber explains that cracks are most likely to spread from chips and small imperfections.

You turn on the heat and a crack appears. It was waiting for something to push it. The defroster supplied the push.

The right way to warm your windshield

  1. Start on low. Let the glass warm slowly and evenly.
  2. Raise the heat in steps. Move up a notch every minute or so instead of jumping to full blast.
  3. Give it extra time. A few more minutes on a freezing morning is cheap.
  4. Skip the shortcuts. No hot water on the glass. Read why in our guide on hot water on a frozen windshield.

Signs your chip is spreading

Look at your glass in good light after a cold start. These are the warning signs:

  • A thin line leaving the chip
  • A crack that grew after you ran the heat
  • A second line forming off the first
  • A line that reaches the edge of the glass

Any of these means call soon. A crack that touches the edge keeps growing. Replacement is the usual answer there.

Fix chips before winter

Caliber's advice is simple: fix small chips and cracks before winter, since they are the weak points that grow. We agree.

A repair keeps your original glass in place. That matters on cars with a camera behind the windshield, because a replacement may need a recalibration afterward. See our page on chip and crack repair. Our chip guide shows the size limits.

When a crack already runs across the glass

We replace rather than repair when a crack runs longer than about six inches, sits in the driver's line of sight, reaches the edge, or comes from three or more impact points.

Why do cracks show up right after I turn on the heat?+
Hot air warms the inside of the glass while the outside stays frozen. That gap stresses the glass, and a chip or flaw gives a crack a place to start.
Should I use the defroster on a cracked windshield?+
Yes, but gently. Start on low and raise the heat in steps. If the crack is long or sits in your view, get it checked soon.
How long should I warm up the car?+
There is no single number. It depends on how cold it is. Keep the heat low at first and let the glass warm evenly before you raise it.
Is a chip worth fixing if it is only a small one?+
Yes. Small chips are the cheapest to repair and the easiest to stop. Waiting gives temperature swings and dirt time to make it worse.
